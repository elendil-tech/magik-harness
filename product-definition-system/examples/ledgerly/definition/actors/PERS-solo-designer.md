## Day in the life

Mara works from home for four to six clients at a time. Month end is invoice day: she copies last month's document, changes the dates and amounts, exports a PDF and emails it. Two weeks later she checks her bank account, and if nothing has arrived she writes a polite reminder she dreads sending. Composite of three interviewees.
