## Finding

Median wait between sending an invoice and receiving money: 38 days. Ten of twelve named late payment as their most frequent frustration. Nine invoiced from a spreadsheet or document template; three used a bookkeeping suite and found it heavy. Seven said clients would pay sooner if paying were one click.

## Limits

Twelve people recruited from communities where the problem is discussed, so they over-represent people who already feel it. Self-reported waits, not measured. All EU-based and mostly in design and development, so no evidence about other trades or other regions.
