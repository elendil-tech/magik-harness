The owner decides why a client's name and address are held, so Ledgerly acts for the owner. A client may still ask to see, correct or erase what is held about them, and sent invoices must be kept for seven years for tax ([[OBL-tax-and-invoicing]]). We have not decided what Ledgerly provides to the owner to answer such a request, nor how the legal retention and an erasure request are reconciled.
