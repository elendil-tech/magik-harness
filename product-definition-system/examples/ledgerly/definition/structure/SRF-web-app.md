## Purpose

Where the work happens: drafting and sending invoices, watching them get paid, managing clients and the plan. Everything in it needs a signed-in member of the workspace.

## Navigation model

A workspace switcher at the top left, then Invoices, Clients and Settings. The Invoices list is the home page. An invoice opens full width; sending and payment status are shown on it rather than in a separate area.
