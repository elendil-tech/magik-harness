## Situation

Ten of the twelve people interviewed named late payment as their most frequent frustration. The cause they gave was rarely that the client refused: the invoice was unclear, sat in a spam folder, or needed a bank transfer the client postponed. Chasing is unpaid work and feels awkward with a client they want to keep.
